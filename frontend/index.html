How JharInnovate Operates

From citizen complaint to ground-level deployment through structured collaboration.

01

01 Report

Citizens, Panchayats, or ULBs report local problems with location data and evidence photos.

02

02 Analyze

AI engine classifies category, detects duplicate issues, and prioritizes urgent community needs.

03

03 Match

System automatically routes problem statements to matching university labs and faculty mentors.

04

04 Collaborate

Student teams co-develop prototypes backed by industry mentors, MSMEs, and government funding.

05

05 Deploy

Field testing, pilot validation, and scale deployment for tangible societal transformation.
