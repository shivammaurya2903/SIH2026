HOW IT WORKS

02

AI categorizes, prioritizes and identifies potentially related challenges.

03

Match

Validated challenges are matched with universities based on expertise.

04

Students, faculty and industry partners work together to develop solutions.

05

Deploy

Tested solutions move into pilots, implementation and measurable impact.
